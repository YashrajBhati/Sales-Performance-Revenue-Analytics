# 📊 Sales Performance & Revenue Analytics

An end-to-end Sales Analytics project using **Python, SQL (SQLite), and Microsoft Power BI** to analyze sales performance, revenue, profitability, customer behavior, and regional performance.

The project focuses on transforming sales data into meaningful business insights and actionable recommendations to support better business decision-making.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Sales performance analysis helps businesses understand their revenue trends, identify profitable products, evaluate customer performance, and recognize areas where profitability can be improved.

In this project, I performed data analysis using Python and SQL, stored and queried data using SQLite, and developed an interactive four-page Power BI dashboard to present key business metrics and insights.

### 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and revenue performance.
- Identify top-performing products and categories.
- Evaluate product-wise profitability and loss-making products.
- Analyze customer purchasing behavior.
- Compare sales and profit across different regions.
- Identify opportunities for revenue growth and profit optimization.
- Provide data-driven business recommendations.

## ⚙️ Project Workflow

The project follows a data analytics workflow:

**Dataset → Python Analysis → SQLite Database & SQL Queries → Power BI Dashboard → Business Insights**

### 1. Python Data Analysis

Python and Pandas were used to work with the sales dataset and perform data analysis.

Key activities included:

- Loading the sales dataset.
- Exploring dataset structure and records.
- Checking column data types.
- Working with date columns.
- Analyzing sales and profit.
- Grouping data by products, customers, and regions.
- Identifying top-performing and loss-making products.

### 2. SQL Analysis Using SQLite

SQL queries were written and executed as part of the Python analysis workflow.

The SQLite database was used to store and query the sales data.

Analysis focused on:

- Total revenue and profit.
- Regional sales performance.
- Product-wise revenue and profitability.
- Customer-wise revenue analysis.
- Identification of loss-making products.

The SQLite database is included in the project repository.

### 3. Power BI Dashboard Development

Microsoft Power BI was used to develop a four-page interactive dashboard.

The dashboard presents business performance through KPI cards, charts, tables, and a region slicer.

## 🔍 Key Business Findings

### 1. Product Performance

The analysis identified the following top products by revenue:

| Product | Revenue |
|---|---:|
| Gaming Laptop X | $538,332.42 |
| Smartphone Z | $363,418.37 |
| Laptop Pro 15 | $303,262.02 |
| Laptop Air 13 | $302,356.62 |
| Conference Table | $202,434.67 |

### 2. Loss-Making Products

Some products generated revenue but also experienced losses.

| Product | Profit |
|---|---:|
| Gaming Laptop X | -$59,695.77 |
| Conference Table | -$31,770.85 |
| Sofa 3-Seater | -$25,126.44 |

This highlights the importance of analyzing profitability rather than evaluating products only by revenue.

### 3. Regional Performance

Regional analysis identified differences in revenue and profit across five regions:

- East
- North
- West
- South
- Central

East recorded the highest revenue and profit among the five regions in the completed analysis.

### 4. Customer Performance

Customer analysis helped identify high-revenue customers and compare customer-level revenue, profit, and order counts.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations were identified:

### Profit Optimization

- Review pricing strategies for low-profit products.
- Investigate losses in Gaming Laptop X.
- Reassess costs of Conference Table and Sofa 3-Seater.
- Optimize discounts to protect profit margins.
- Monitor product-wise profitability regularly.

### Revenue Growth Opportunities

- Focus on top-performing products to increase sales.
- Target high-value customers with personalized offers.
- Strengthen sales in high-revenue regions.
- Introduce cross-selling and upselling strategies.
- Improve customer retention through loyalty programs.

### Strategic Recommendations

- Increase revenue by focusing on high-performing products and profitable customer segments.
- Reduce losses by reviewing pricing and production costs of loss-making products.
- Improve customer retention through loyalty programs and personalized offers.
- Optimize regional performance through stronger sales strategies.
- Regularly monitor revenue, expenses, and profit.
